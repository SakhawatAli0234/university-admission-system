System Features

Everything You Need in One Portal

The system manages the complete admission and entrance test process from student registration to final results.

Student Registration

Students can create accounts and securely provide their personal and academic information.

Admission Applications

Complete and submit applications while selecting the desired degree program and department.

Online Entrance Test

Take online entrance examinations with timed questions and automatic submission.

Automatic Results

Test marks, percentages, pass or fail status, and merit information can be displayed instantly.

Application Tracking

Students can monitor application progress and admission decisions from their dashboard.

Notifications

Important admission deadlines, test schedules, results, and system updates are shown to students.
